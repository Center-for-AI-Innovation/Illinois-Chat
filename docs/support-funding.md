# Support & Funding

Illinois Chat is developed by the [Center for AI Innovation (CAII)](https://ai.ncsa.illinois.edu/) at the [National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA)](https://ncsa.illinois.edu/), University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Its development and the hosted site at [chat.illinois.edu](https://chat.illinois.edu) are made possible by the organizations below, among others.

## Illinois Computes

[Illinois Computes](https://computes.illinois.edu/) is a University of Illinois program that gives researchers in every discipline no-cost access to computing and storage resources, consultations, and staff expertise.

Illinois Chat is one of the Illinois-built LLM services supported through Illinois Computes, alongside Lumen, LLM Hub, and LLMFlux. Like other Illinois Computes resources, it is free to use. The [Which LLM service is right for you?](https://llm.computes.illinois.edu/) guide compares the four services and helps you pick the one that fits your work.

## Funding for individual chatbots

Chatbots built on the platform may have funding of their own. For example, [CropWizard](use-cases/cropwizard/index.md#funding-and-acknowledgements) is funded through the AIFARMS national AI institute and its industry partners.
