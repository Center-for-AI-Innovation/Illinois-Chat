# Finding Chatbots

The **Chatbots Hub** at [chat.illinois.edu/chatbots](https://chat.illinois.edu/chatbots) lists the chatbots you can use. Sign in to see the ones shared with you.

## Browsing

The hub groups chatbots into sections:

- **Your Bots** — chatbots you own, administer, or are a member of, whatever their access level.
- **Course Assistants**, **Department Resources** and **Public Bots** — public chatbots, grouped by the project type their builders tagged them with.

The **Featured** row shows a random selection of twenty public chatbots.

## Searching and filtering

Use the search bar ("Search for chatbots...") to find a chatbot by name or description. The filter panel narrows the list by **category**, by **privacy**, and with **Show My Bots**, which limits the list to chatbots you have a role in.

Categories come from the tags builders set on their chatbot's Dashboard (project type and organization). To create your own, choose **Create Your Own Bot** and follow the [Quickstart](quickstart.md).
